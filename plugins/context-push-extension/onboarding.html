Before Mana starts reading your pages

This extension lets Mana see the page (or video captions) you're currently looking at, so she can answer things like "what does this say" or "what am I watching" -- only when you actually ask something that sounds like it's about the current page.

What this means:

  • Once enabled, it reads page text continuously, not just when you ask -- like a webcam, always-on but always visible when it's active.
  • Whenever it's active, the toolbar icon shows a clear indicator: green = reading -- grey means off.
  • A single click on the toolbar icon turns it off completely, and it stays off until you click it again -- even across browser restarts.
  • Nothing is stored permanently -- Mana only ever holds the most recent page for 2 minutes, and it's never written to her memory.
  • Bilibili isn't supported, and this only reads visible caption text on YouTube, not audio/video itself.

This is one of the more invasive things Mana can do -- it can see whatever page you have open. The indicator and off switch above are the safeguard: you can always tell it's on, and always turn it off in one click.
